How to play

Goal

Destroy the enemy King. There are no turns: both sides move at the same time.

Moving

Select one of your pieces, then a target. grey dots are moves, red dots are attacks. Pieces slide square by square in real time; tap the red × on a moving piece to stop it.

After each move you wait for your cooldown (meter at the bottom right) before the next one. Select and look at pieces any time.

Combat

A piece that runs into an enemy stops and deals 10 HP per second for as long as it stays. Several attackers add up. Health bars go green → orange → red.

Pawns

Pawns walk forward (two squares on their first move) but only attack diagonally. A pawn reaching the far side is promoted.

Options

Formation defense: a piece on a square your other pieces could move to takes half damage per guard, shown as small shields. Promotion: auto-Queen or pick the piece yourself.
